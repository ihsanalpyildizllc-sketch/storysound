Fill out a 2-minute quiz about who the song is for — the name, occasion, a few memories, how you want it to feel. We write and record the full song, then show you a free 60-second preview right in your browser. Love it? Unlock the full song for $39. Don't love it? Walk away, no charge.

What genres can I choose from?

Pop, R&B/Soul, Country, Hip-Hop/Rap, Reggaeton, Jazz, Folk/Acoustic, Dance/EDM, Gospel/Inspirational, and more. Choose any style — the song is written and produced in that genre specifically.

How long does it take?

Most songs are ready in 2–4 minutes. You watch a live progress bar while we write and record. The preview appears automatically the moment it's done — no email, no waiting, no refresh needed.

What if I don't love the song?

You have unlimited re-dos until it's perfect. If after multiple attempts we still can't nail it, you get a full refund — no questions asked. We've never had to issue more than one revision request per order.

Is this AI or real musicians?

Our lyrics are written by AI trained on real emotional storytelling, and the music is produced at studio quality. The result sounds like a real artist recorded it — because the production quality meets that standard. You're getting the personalization of a human songwriter at a fraction of the price and in minutes instead of days.

What do I get after I pay?

The full song — MP3 download, a shareable private link, and access to your personal delivery page where you can request changes, add printable lyrics, or create a music video version of your gift.
