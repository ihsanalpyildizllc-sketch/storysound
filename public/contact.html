Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a revision?

Reply to your delivery email with what you'd like changed, or email support@getstoory.com with your order ID.

My song hasn't arrived — what do I do?

First, check your spam folder. If you still can't find it, email us with your order email address and we'll look it up and resend immediately.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — we offer a 100% money-back guarantee. See our Refund Policy for full details.

How long does delivery take?

The preview is ready in your browser within 2–5 minutes of submitting. Full delivery is 30 minutes or 48 hours depending on the plan you selected at checkout.
