Questions you might have

What happens if I cancel?

If you cancel within 7 days, you pay nothing. If you cancel after your year begins, you keep access through the end of your paid year. After that, your song link will revert to a 90-day standard link.

When do I get my free songs?

Your first free song is available immediately when you join. The second unlocks after your year begins on Day 8. Both are full custom songs — same quality, same process, yours to keep or gift.

Is the $99 price locked in?

Yes. Founding Vault members are price-locked at $99/year for as long as they remain members. If we ever raise the price for new members, yours stays the same.

What does "permanent" actually mean?

Your song lives in our Vault — backed up, studio-quality, with a private link that never changes. As long as your Vault membership is active, your song is there. Forever is a strong word, but we mean it.

Can I gift my free songs to someone else?

Absolutely — that's the whole idea. Your free monthly songs can be for anyone. A parent, a partner, a friend. You fill out their story and we compose it. Their song, their permanent link, their gift.
