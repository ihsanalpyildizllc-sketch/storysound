Questions

Everything you're wondering

What if it comes across as cheesy?
The line between cheesy and moving is specificity. Generic love songs make people wince because they could be about anyone. Yours is built from real names, real memories, and moments only the two of you would recognise. That's the part that makes people cry.
What if I don't like the full song?
The preview you just heard comes from the same recording. Unlocking gives you the complete track — every verse, the chorus and the bridge, at the same quality. If the style, the voice or the lyrics aren't right, we'll redo it free until it feels perfect.
What if I'm not happy with the final result?
There's a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're still not happy after revisions, you get a full refund — no questions asked.
Is my payment secure?
Checkout runs on an encrypted, PCI-compliant payment system. All major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted. Your card details are encrypted and never stored on our servers.
How quickly do I get the full song after paying?
About 30 minutes. The moment you unlock it and we finalise the track, you get full access to stream, download and share it.
How do I share the song with them?
You'll get a private link you can text, email, or open on any device. Most people play it in person — at dinner, in the car, or in a quiet moment together. You can also download the HD file to play through a speaker, drop into a slideshow, or simply keep forever.
What happens if I don't unlock the full song right now?
Your preview is held for a limited time. After that it's archived and our songwriters move on to new orders. Wanting changes or the full song later may mean recreating it from scratch — a longer wait, with no guarantee of the same result or the same price. The best version of your song is ready right now.
